# Video-Guidelines

## Check the remote viewer’s feed before a hybrid session

Ask a volunteer to join the attendee-facing stream on a second device. Choose one detail the audience needs to identify, such as a line of code, a chart label, or a small object under a demo camera. Check it again after switching from slides to the live demo or camera.
If the viewer cannot identify the detail, enlarge it, adjust the crop, or switch to a labeled still image. Repeat the check before the session starts.

Optional [hybrid pointing rehearsal record](https://light-tracer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hybrid-pointing-rehearsal-record-open-v1.pdf) (PDF, CC BY 4.0) to record what the viewer saw.
